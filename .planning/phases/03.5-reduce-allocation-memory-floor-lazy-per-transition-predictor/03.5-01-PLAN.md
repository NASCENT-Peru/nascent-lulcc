<objective>
Add scoped multi-threaded ranger prediction (Goal 2 of Phase 3.5) so the per-transition `predict` step — the dominant cost in the Andes `setup_inputs` loop (~5.7 h of the 20665s baseline) — can use spare cores while leaving every other native thread pool pinned to 1.

Purpose: The 571309 baseline (BAU, selva_andina) is memory-bound with idle cores; `stage=predict` dominates wall time. Letting ranger parallelise over trees (`num.threads > 1`), bounded by `threads × workers ≤ cores` and scoped to the predict call only, cuts the prediction wall time without un-pinning BLAS/arrow/data.table (which would risk the documented Lustre IO-thread deadlock at src/allocation.r L210-214 and oversubscription).

Output: A new `get_allocation_predict_num_threads()` resolver, `num.threads` applied at BOTH predict call sites (mlr3 learner `param_set` and the direct ranger fallback), a smoke-script env-knob echo, and unit coverage for the resolver math.
</objective>
