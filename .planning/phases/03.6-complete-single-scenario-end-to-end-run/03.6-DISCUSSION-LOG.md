**User's choice:** Per-region parallel jobs on highmem/fat ~256 GB nodes (1 region/node), national
mosaic assembled post-hoc for every timestep, fanned out by a thin launcher script + dependent
mosaic-assembly job.
**Notes:** Choice made with the explicit caveat surfaced in discussion — per-region parallelism
collides on the region-agnostic `posterior_<year>.tif` mosaic path (`src/allocation.r:1804`) and
requires decoupling per-region chaining from the shared mosaic write (CONTEXT.md D-05).
