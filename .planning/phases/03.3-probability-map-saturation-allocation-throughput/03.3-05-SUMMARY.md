# Summary — 03.3-05 (operator gate)

Closure of the Phase 3.3 Wave-4 operator gate.

## What was delivered

- **ALLOC-06..10 are present in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`** (verified).
- **Operator-gate live HPC verification:** satisfied by the andes full-pipeline run
  (jobs 573721 / 573776, BAU × andes × 2022→2026 on a fat-exclusive node). The run
  exercised the full saturation path end to end:
  - `src/saturation_diagnostics.r` ran inline and wrote `saturation_summary.csv`
    (24 rows).
  - `AUDIT stage=saturation … active=24 met=22 exempt=0 failed=2 threshold=0.90` —
    22 of 24 transitions met the 0.90 saturation threshold.
  - Dinamica Expander/Patcher allocated from the probability cube and produced
    `posterior.tif` (`DINAMICA_EXIT status=0`) — i.e. real allocation throughput, not
    the ~1% placement that originally motivated this phase.

## Closure rationale

The operator gate's intent — prove on live HPC that the saturation machinery (Plans
01–04) produces probability maps dense enough for Dinamica to allocate — is met by the
andes run. The original gate text said "all Peruvian regions"; per the operator, the
other regions already produced probability maps via the smoke, and andes (the largest
forest region) was the final validation case. The 2 sub-threshold transitions are a
modeling/saturation diagnostic to track, not a Phase 3.3 blocker.

## Follow-ups

- The 2 transitions below the 0.90 saturation threshold are candidates for the
  calibration/post-processing work tracked under Phase 4 / the full-run phase (3.6).
