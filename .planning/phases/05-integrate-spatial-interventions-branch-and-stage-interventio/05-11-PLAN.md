<objective>
Close WR-01, WR-02, WR-03 and IN-04 — the adjustment-semantics defects inside
`relative_prob_adjust()` and `absolute_prob_adjust()`.

WR-01: the percentile mean is computed with `>=` but the rows to adjust are selected with strict
`>`, so when probabilities tie at the percentile — exactly what happens after a higher-ranked
Absolute intervention flattens a zone, and after the upstream `tot_prob` clamp — zero rows are
selected while the mean was taken over many; the AUDIT line still counts the intervention as
applied. The same function has an exact-zero dead zone: at `Perc_diff == 0` neither the Increase nor
the Decrease branch runs, so `Prob_adjust_threshold` — whose entire purpose is to guarantee a
minimum nudge when the zones are indistinguishable — is silently skipped, while
`Increase_inside_decrease_outside` does apply it. All four shipped scenarios rely on
`Prob_adjust_threshold: 5` doing that job.

WR-02: both helpers clamp the *entire* probability table inside the per-target-class loop, so an
intervention declared for one class rewrites rows of every other transition, uncounted by
`rows_changed`. That contradicts the phase's recorded non-renormalisation decision.

Purpose: make the engine's arithmetic match its own audit numbers and its own documented design.

Output: aligned comparisons, a zero-difference path, scoped clamps, honest threshold logging, and
fixtures that reproduce each defect.
</objective>
