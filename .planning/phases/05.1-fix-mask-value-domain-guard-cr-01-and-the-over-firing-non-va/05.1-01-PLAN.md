<objective>
Close the runtime half of CR-01: `.mask_inside_lut()` currently decides membership with
`lut[cid[!is.na(v) & v == 1]] <- TRUE` and never checks that `v` is in `{0, 1, NA}`. A mask
re-burned with 255 (the `gdal_rasterize` / QGIS 8-bit default), or converted to a categorical
GeoTIFF, reads as **entirely outside** with no error and no log line. For the two shipped
`Absolute` / `0` / `Outside` entries (`Mining_freeze_post_2030` in `config/NAT_interventions.yml`
and the `low_es_value_mask.tif` entry in `config/SOC_interventions.yml`) the zone is the
*complement* of the mask, so an all-FALSE LUT inverts the policy onto the whole region and
silently zeroes every `* -> mining` probability from 2032 onward.

This plan adds the fatal runtime guard (D-01) and the cheap pre-flight half (D-02), plus the
fixtures that prove each branch fires and that a correct 1-coded mask does not regress.

Purpose: a non-conforming mask must abort the run, not produce a wrong scientific result that
the smoke verifier reports as PASS.
Output: two guarded source files and four new/updated test blocks across two test files.
</objective>
