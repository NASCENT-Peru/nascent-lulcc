<objective>
Make the existing allocation driver safe to run unfiltered across the full timestep schedule
AND under per-region parallel jobs. Three coupled edits to `src/allocation.r`:

1. **D-11 schedule reconcile** — replace the `step_length`-based `seq()` (ends 2058, 9 steps)
   with the authoritative `config[["simulation_year_steps"]]` (`[2022,2024,2028,…,2060]`, 10
   steps), so the loop requests only the `year_ant` rate tables that were actually generated.
2. **D-05 per-region chaining decouple** — when `ALLOCATION_REGION_FILTER` scopes a single
   region, chain each timestep on that region's OWN `region_<suffix>/posterior.tif` instead of
   the region-agnostic national mosaic `posterior_<year>.tif`, and do NOT have the per-region
   job write the shared national mosaic (assembly moves to the post-hoc job in Plan 03).
3. **D-10 atomic writes** — every per-timestep `terra::writeRaster` writes to a `.tmp.tif` path
   then `file.rename()` into place, so a killed job cannot leave a half-written posterior that
   the Plan 02 resume mistakes for complete.

Purpose: This is the foundational correctness work. The launcher (Plan 02) chains on the
decoupled per-region posterior; the manifest (Plan 04) inventories atomic-written posteriors;
the mosaic assembler (Plan 03) becomes the sole writer of the national path. All downstream
plans depend on this contract being fixed.
Output: Modified `src/allocation.r` (driver loop + timestep function + anterior-write site).
</objective>
