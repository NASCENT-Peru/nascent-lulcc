<objective>
Lift the existing per-timestep mosaic logic (currently inline in `run_allocation_one_timestep`,
`src/allocation.r:1787-1815`) into a standalone post-hoc assembler that, for EVERY timestep in
`simulation_year_steps`, collects the per-region `region_<suffix>/posterior.tif` files and
merges/extends them into a national `posterior_<year>.tif`. Under per-region parallel jobs the
single-region driver path (Plan 01 D-05) no longer writes the national mosaic, so this assembler
becomes the SOLE writer of `posterior_<year>.tif` (D-03), producing the full national time series.

Purpose: Decouple national-mosaic production from the per-region serial chains so regions can run
concurrently without clobbering a shared path. The assembler is the `afterok` target the Plan 02
launcher queues after all region jobs finish.
Output: `scripts/assemble_national_mosaic.r` (the assembler) + `scripts/submit_assemble_mosaic.sh`
(thin SBATCH wrapper).
</objective>

| killed assembler → national mosaic | An assembler killed mid-write could leave a half-written posterior_<year>.tif. |
