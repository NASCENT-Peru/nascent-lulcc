GAP-1: `scripts/verify_intervention_smoke.r:335-338` prints one `Info:` line and `next`s when an
Absolute-0 intervention has no matching rows in `trans_rates.csv`. That `next` sits ABOVE the D-04

config shape: `config/NAT_interventions.yml` pairs

has rows) with `Mining_freeze_post_2030` (Absolute/0/Outside, `Transition_target_classes: mining`
alone). Any region/year with no `* -> mining` transition row silently skips the mining freeze's
D-04 while the green banner is read as evidence for both — CR-01's observational blind spot,
re-opened.

R51-WR-04: the `n_zone == 0` INFO downgrade (`:396-403`) also swallows a probability map that is
entirely NA over the region. The review proposed failing unconditionally on that shape. **That
would plant a false FAIL**: `src/allocation.r:3210-3237` deliberately writes an all-NA TIF for an
active transition with no predictions (Phase 3.3 Pitfall 2 — the `%03d` sequence Dinamica binds to
must stay gap-free) and logs `WARN id_trans=<id> row=<k> has no predictions; wrote empty TIF`. So
the correct closure is to DISCRIMINATE: an empty map explained by that WARN line stays INFO; an
unexplained empty map FAILs. This plan implements the discrimination, not the review's unconditional
fail.

R51-IN-01: the pre-flight wording `intervention mask: <path> has value(s) outside {0,1,NA} (...)`
matches two forbidden markers at once, so one bad mask yields two `fail()` entries and inflates
`errors=N`.

Purpose: a PASS banner must mean every active Absolute-0 intervention was actually asserted on.
Output: a restructured section (c) whose assertions no longer depend on `trans_rates.csv` content,
a de-duplicated section (d), and multi-intervention fixtures — the fixture shape whose absence is
the stated reason GAP-1 shipped.
